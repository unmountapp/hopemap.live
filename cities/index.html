For Cities and Agencies

HopeMap gives your city a free, real-time map of shelter, food and mental-health resources — in 54 languages, working offline, and built to fit your system.

What your city gets

Free tools. Your data. Open code.

🖥️ Free Dashboard

A free dashboard for the city and its providers to manage listings, post live status, and see how resources are being found.

🔒 Control Your Listings

Your city and providers control your own listings — hours, capacity, services. No one else edits them.

📂 Open Source

Open-source code for any grant-funded work. Your city's investment is portable, auditable and reusable.

📋 Team Briefing

A short briefing for your team — 20 minutes, no jargon, covering what HopeMap does, how it fits your system, and next steps.

How status stays current

Provider-verified, community-supported, human-reviewed.

  • Verified providers post open or closed status, bed counts, food supply and wait times
  • Community reports fill gaps and are weighted by each reporter's accuracy history
  • Listings get human review to catch errors and keep data clean
Privacy

How we handle data.

HopeMap does not collect personal information when someone searches. No account is needed, no location is stored, and no search history is kept. Status reports posted by providers and community members are visible to everyone using the map.
